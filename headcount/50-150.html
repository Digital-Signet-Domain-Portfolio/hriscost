HRIS cost for 50-150 employees

At 100 employees, the published HRIS plans cost $12,000 a year on BambooHR Core ($10 per employee per month), $20,400 on BambooHR Pro and $15,360 on Gusto Plus ($80 a month + $12 per person), at list before volume discounts and add-ons. Rippling publishes only a floor of $8 per employee per month plus a base fee; HiBob and Paylocity publish no price, so they enter the comparison through a quote.

Reading the table

The HRIS plans (BambooHR, Gusto, Deel, Zoho People) are the like-for-like rows. Justworks PEO rows include payroll, HR services and benefits administration, with Justworks as co-employer, so they sit far above an HRIS seat and should be compared with a PEO quote rather than with BambooHR. Justworks Payroll, at $8 per employee per month plus $50 a month, is its HRIS-comparable plan.

Modules a 100-person company usually prices

  • Core HRIS (records, org chart, directory, time off)
  • Payroll (multi-state if staff work in several states)
  • Recruiting (ATS)
  • Performance (review cycles, goals)
  • Benefits administration (depends on your broker arrangement)
  • Time tracking (depends on how many staff are hourly)

Each vendor packages these differently: some in plan tiers, some as add-ons priced in the quote. Get every add-on as a per-employee line and enter the total in the ledger.

Implementation and renewal

None of these vendors publishes an implementation fee as a price (see implementation fees), so take it from your quote. The renewal increase is set by your contract; the renewal page shows what 3%, 5% or 10% a year adds over 3 years.
