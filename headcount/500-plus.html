Enterprise HRIS cost (500+ employees)

For an enterprise HRIS pricing comparison, none of Workday, UKG, ADP Workforce Now, Paycom or Paylocity shows a price on its own website, and Rippling publishes only a floor of $8 per employee per month plus a base fee. Two public documents give list prices: Workday's UK G-Cloud 15 pricing document prices Core HCM + Global Payroll Connect at £163.22 per employee per year for 750 to 1,500 employees, falling by band to £59.35 above 50,000 (excluding VAT), and Vertosoft, a UKG reseller, lists UKG Pro Pay and People Center at a $22.00 MSRP per employee per month in its South Carolina state-contract price book. The published plans that run at this size give the reference points: at 1,000 employees, BambooHR lists at $120,000 to $300,000 a year (Core to Elite) and Deel HR at $60,000 to $348,000, before volume discounts. Neither public list includes implementation, so price it alongside the subscription.

What an enterprise quote has to cover

  • Subscription: per-employee rate by module (core HCM, payroll, workforce management, recruiting, learning, compensation), and how the rate changes with headcount bands.
  • Implementation: ask whether the vendor or a partner firm implements, and for a fixed fee and a written scope. Compare it with the year-1 subscription.
  • Term and renewal: multi-year terms are common at this size. Get the annual renewal cap and the price for headcount growth in writing (see renewal increases).
  • Countries: payroll and compliance per country are often separate lines.

Comparing enterprise quotes

Enter each vendor's subscription under “Your quote” in the ledger at your headcount, add the implementation fee and the renewal cap from its contract, and compare the 3-year totals. A quote with a lower rate but a larger implementation fee or an uncapped renewal can cost more over the term.

When is Rippling an enterprise option?

For companies that want HR, payroll, IT and device management on one platform and prefer a product-led rollout to a partner-led implementation. Rippling's published $8 floor excludes its base fee and modules, so price the full stack before comparing it with Workday or UKG.
