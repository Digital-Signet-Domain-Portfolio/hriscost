Mid-market HRIS cost (150-500 employees)

At 250 employees, BambooHR's published plans cost $30,000 (Core) to $75,000 (Elite) a year at list, before the volume discounts BambooHR applies as headcount grows. Most of the vendors mid-market buyers shortlist, Paylocity, Paycom, HiBob, ADP Workforce Now and Namely, show no price on their own sites, and Rippling publishes only a floor of $8 per employee per month plus a base fee. So a mid-market comparison is mostly a comparison of quotes: the table gives the published reference points, and the ledger takes each quote.

Getting quotes you can compare

Quote-only vendors package modules differently, so two quotes for the same headcount rarely line up as sent. Ask every vendor for the same breakdown:

  • Per-employee rate for each module, as separate lines
  • Any monthly base or platform fee
  • Any per-payroll-run or year-end (W-2) charge, converted to a monthly figure
  • Implementation as a fixed fee with a written scope
  • Contract term, seat minimum and a written annual renewal cap

Then enter each quote under “Your quote” in the ledger at your headcount, with the same add-ons, implementation and renewal cap, to set them side by side over 3 years.

Who sits on the buying committee

At this size the decision usually involves finance (budget owner), the head of People (system owner) and IT or security (sign-off on access, SSO and data handling). Bring all three into the quote stage: SSO, API access and data-export terms are easier to settle before signature.
