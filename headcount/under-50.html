HRIS cost for under 50 employees

For under-50 employee deployments in 2026, the cheapest fully-featured HRIS is Zoho People Essential HR at $1.50/user/mo, followed by Deel HR free (up to 200) and Remote HR free. For US-payroll-heavy buyers, Gusto Simple at $49 + $6 PEPM is the published winner. A 25-person startup should budget $4,000-$15,000 for year-1 HRIS spend.

Is the free tier of Deel or Remote really enough?

Yes for HR core (directory, time-off, document storage, org chart). Both add paid lines for EOR, contractor management, and global payroll. If you need US payroll and you are not running a global contractor team, Deel and Remote free tiers will leave you needing Gusto or Rippling for payroll anyway.

Year-1 budget for a 25-person startup

Realistic budget for a US-only 25-person startup on Gusto Plus + benefits: $5,000-$8,000 year-1 software, $0-$1,500 implementation, $0 broker fee (Gusto-bundled benefits). Total: $5,000-$10,000. For a globally-distributed startup, swap Gusto for Deel (free HR + per-contractor fees) and budget $3,000-$6,000 in contractor fees instead.

When do you outgrow each option?

  • Zoho People: outgrown at 100 employees when ecosystem-fit pressure forces an upgrade to BambooHR or Rippling.
  • Gusto Simple: outgrown at 30 employees when Performance and advanced PTO matter.
  • Deel HR free: outgrown at 200 employees (free tier cap) or earlier if HRIS depth becomes the binding constraint.
  • Remote HR free: no cap on the free tier, but HRIS depth becomes the binding constraint at 100+ employees.
