HRIS cost for under 50 employees

The free-HRIS route closed in 2026. Deel and Remote no longer publish a free HR tier, so the cheapest published option for an under-50 team is Zoho People Essential HR at $3/user/month billed annually on the US plan line-up, then Deel HR Core at $5 per employee per month. For US payroll, Gusto Simple at $49 a month + $6 per person is the lowest published payroll-and-HR price. At 25 employees, list prices come to $900 a year on Zoho People, $1,500 on Deel HR Core and $2,388 on Gusto Simple.

What happened to the Deel and Remote free HR tiers?

Both are gone from the published rate cards. Deel now sells Deel HR as a three-tier paid ladder starting at Core, $5 per employee per month, with Advanced at $19 and Elite at $29. Remote publishes no free plan and no standalone HRIS rate at all: its HR Core is bundled into whichever paid product you buy, so the cheapest published route onto the platform is Global Payroll from $19 per employee per month or Contractor Management at $29 per contractor per month. If you shortlisted either vendor on the strength of a free HR tier, that reason has expired.

When do you outgrow each option?

  • Zoho People: Essential HR covers core records and time off; Workforce, Talent and Enterprise add attendance, performance and learning at $5 to $8 per user.
  • Gusto Simple: outgrown when you need the HR tools that start on Plus ($80 a month + $12 per person).
  • Deel HR Core: outgrown when performance reviews, goals or LMS become requirements, which moves you to Advanced at $19 per employee per month, a near four-fold step up.
  • Remote: never a cheap HRIS-only option now that the free tier is gone. It earns its place only when you are already buying Remote Payroll, EOR or Contractor Management and HR Core arrives bundled.
