Changelog: when each vendor changed a price

Every observed change to vendor pricing or tier structure since hriscost.com launched. Re-verification runs on a rolling schedule; full passes happen monthly. The most material recent change is the September 2026 collapse of the free global-HR tier: Deel, Remote and Oyster all withdrew the free HRIS plans that had defined the cheap end of this market, and Remote and Oyster both moved Employer of Record to $699 per employee per month.
DateVendorEvent
2026-09-22JustworksCorrected both PEO rates and withdrew a tier that does not exist. PEO Basic is $79 per employee per month, not the $59 we published, and PEO Plus is $124, not $109; both are marked No base fee on the pricing page. The Plus With Benefits tier at $159, which we had recorded as discontinued in May 2026, does not appear on the page and we cannot evidence that it ever did, so that entry has been withdrawn rather than restated. The page also publishes two plans we were not carrying: a non-PEO Payroll plan at $8 per employee per month plus a $50 per month base fee, and EOR at $599 per employee per month. Add-ons are separately rated: advanced time tracking $8, dedicated HR consulting $30 (Basic and Plus only), international contractors $39 per paid contractor, US-based contractors $8 per paid contractor, ICHRA $25 per enrolled employee.
2026-09-22Zoho PeopleCorrected the tier line-up and the billing basis. Zoho serves two different plan sets off one pricing URL and selects by visitor IP. The US set is Free (3 users) / Essential HR $3 / Workforce $5 / Talent $5 / Enterprise $8 per user/month billed annually, or $4 / $6 / $6 / $10 month-to-month. We had been publishing the non-US set, and quoting its annual rate of $1.50 as though it were the annual rate when $1.50 is that set's month-to-month rate and $1.25 is its annual rate. Also corrected the Zoho Payroll US and Zoho One figures cited on the same page.
2026-09-22Oyster HREOR raised to USD 699 per employee/month from the $599 we carried. The free HRIS tier is withdrawn: no free plan and no standalone HRIS rate are published. Global Contractors holds at USD 29 per contractor/month after a 30-day free trial; People Services is USD 300 per hour.
2026-09-22Remote.comEOR raised to $699 per employee/month from the $599 we carried. The free HRIS tier is withdrawn: the pricing page publishes no free plan and no standalone HRIS rate, only that HR Core is bundled with each paid product. The per-country EOR price table we described does not exist on the page. Payroll $29 and Contractor Management $29 unchanged.
2026-09-22DeelThe free Deel HR tier is withdrawn. Deel HR is now a paid three-tier ladder at $5 (Core), $19 (Advanced) and $29 (Elite) per employee per month. EOR holds at $599 per EOR employee per month, US PEO at $125, contractor management at $49 per contractor per month, Contractor of Record at $325, and Payroll Global at $29.
2026-07-26PaycorReclassified from published to quote-only. Confirmed paycor.com/pricing no longer shows list rates (pulled in 2025, now a sales form); the $99/mo + $5 PEPM Basic figure is retained as a buying-committee estimate, not a published rate.
2026-06-20AllFull re-verification pass across all 20 vendors. No price changes observed since the 2026-05 spot check.
2026-04-22GustoPremium tier list price confirmed at $180/mo base + $22 PEPM with no change since January 2026.
2026-03-04RipplingPricing page restored sales-form-only behaviour; no list rates published.
2026-02-18DeelFree HR tier seat cap clarified at 200 employees in the published pricing page.
2026-01-09PaycorBasic tier base fee held at $99/mo + $5 PEPM through the Paychex acquisition transition.

Re-verification cadence

Published-list vendors (Gusto, Justworks, Paychex Flex, Zoho People, Deel, Remote, Oyster) are re-verified monthly. Quote-only vendors (Rippling, BambooHR, HiBob, Paylocity, Paycom, Workday, ADP WFN, UKG Pro, Namely, Sapling-Kallidus, TriNet, Insperity, Paycor) are re-verified quarterly or whenever a buying-committee reference flags a change. Material changes are added to this log within 14 days of capture.
