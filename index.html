HRIS software cost in 2026

In 2026, HRIS software cost sits at $6-$22 PEPM for vendors with published list pricing (Gusto, Paychex Flex, Zoho People, Deel HR, Remote HR, Oyster HR, Justworks)[1]and $10-$45 PEPM for quote-only mid-market and enterprise vendors (Rippling, BambooHR, HiBob, Paylocity, Paycom, Workday, UKG, ADP). A 100-employee company should budget $14K-$54K in Year 1 including implementation, with a + 12% annual uplift applied as our default renewal assumption across years 2 and 3. Every figure below is sourced to the vendor's pricing page or, where the vendor refuses to publish, to a buying-committee band with explicit disclosure.
