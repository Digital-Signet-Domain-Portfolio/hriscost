HRIS cost FAQ

The 8 highest-volume HRIS cost questions, answered with sourced 2026 figures. Every numeric claim links back to the vendor pricing page or buying-committee reference on /sources.

How much does HRIS software cost per employee per month in 2026?

Published-list vendors: $1.50 (Zoho People Essential HR) to $109 (Justworks Plus PEO). The bulk of mid-market HRIS PEPM sits in $10-$25. Quote-only enterprise vendors (Workday, UKG Pro) run $25-$45 PEPM at 1,000+ employees.

What is the average HRIS implementation fee?

Implementation fees range from $0 (Gusto, self-serve) to $1.5M+ (Workday HCM at enterprise scale with Deloitte). The mid-market central estimate is $3,000-$25,000 for a 100-500 employee deployment.

Why is HRIS pricing so often quote-only?

Three reasons: vendors maximise ASP by quote-gating, sales motions are built around named-account discounts that don't fit list-pricing, and modular bundling (Rippling, Paylocity, Workday) makes a single list price impossible to publish. The exception is the SMB published-list cluster (Gusto, Paychex Flex, Zoho People, Justworks).

What is the typical HRIS contract length and renewal uplift?

1-year MSAs are standard for SMB published vendors (Gusto, Justworks). 3-year MSAs are standard for quote-only mid-market (Rippling, BambooHR, HiBob, Paylocity). 3-5 year for enterprise (Workday, UKG). Default renewal uplift is 12% per year unless explicitly capped in the MSA.

Are there free HRIS options for under 50 employees?

Yes. Deel HR is free up to 200 employees. Remote HR is free at any seat count. Zoho People offers a free plan up to 5 employees. None of the three is free for US payroll; you still need a paid payroll provider on top.

How does PEO pricing differ from standalone HRIS?

PEO admin fees run $59-$180 PEPM and bundle benefits master plan administration, payroll, and HR services. Standalone HRIS PEPM is $4-$45 and does not include benefits broker fees or payroll. The PEO premium can be offset by the benefits-arbitrage on the master health plan in tough rating areas.

Which HRIS vendors gate SAML SSO behind an enterprise tier?

BambooHR (Pro tier only), Gusto (Plus tier and above), ADP Workforce Now (separately quoted). Rippling, Paylocity, Paycom, HiBob, Workday, and UKG Pro all bundle SAML SSO at no separate upcharge.

What hidden fees should be added to the budget?

Off-cycle payroll runs ($25-$100 per run), per-state tax filing ($15/mo per additional state on Gusto Plus), per-payroll-run fees (most quote-only vendors), benefits broker fees (typical 4-8% of premium), Workato or Merge.dev middleware ($1,200-$60,000 year 1), and SSO upcharge where applicable.
