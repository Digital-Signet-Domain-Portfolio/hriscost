About

About hriscost.com

hriscost.com is a single-purpose reference site that publishes a live 3-year HRIS TCO ledger and per-vendor cost analysis for the 20 vendors that matter in the HRIS market in 2026. Built and maintained by Oliver Wakefield-Smith at Digital Signet.

Who built it

Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Founder of Digital Signet. Digital Signet operates a portfolio of vertical cost-reference sites that publish primary-source numbers in narrowly-scoped categories. hriscost.com sits in that portfolio alongside siemcostcalculator, paymentgatewaycost, and itbudgetcalculator.

Editorial standard

Every numeric claim on hriscost.com is either sourced to a primary URL (the vendor's own pricing page) or, for quote-only vendors, published as our own buying-committee estimate with explicit disclosure - a low-mid-high band, never a single fabricated rate. Category references such as sso.tax, NAPEO, and SHRM are cited where relevant for definitions and context. If you spot a stale figure or a figure you can disprove, email and we re-verify within 14 days.

Affiliate and vendor relationships

hriscost.com does not currently carry vendor sponsorship. Some outbound links to vendor pricing pages may at a future date become affiliate links via the Impact network; if and when that happens, the affected pages will carry an inline disclosure. No vendor pays for placement, no rates are influenced by partnerships.

Contact for corrections

oliver@digitalsignet.com is the correction inbox. Include the page URL, the figure, and your source. We re-verify within 14 days and log changes to /changelog.

How the site is funded

Digital Signet funds the site as part of its portfolio. The longer-term funding model is a mix of category-relevant affiliate partnerships and direct sponsorship from non-conflicted services (broker firms, implementation partners, advisory practices) that do not themselves sell HRIS software. We will never carry a paid placement from a vendor we rank or benchmark.
