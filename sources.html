How to audit a specific figure

Open the source URL above for the vendor and compare its pricing page with the figure on our vendor page. If a price has moved since the date shown, send a correction to oliver@digitalsignet.com.

Quote-only vendors

For the 11 vendors that publish no per-employee rate on their own site (Rippling, HiBob, Paylocity, Paycom, Workday, ADP Workforce Now, UKG Pro, Namely, Sapling, TriNet and Insperity), the source above is the page that shows no price is published. We do not estimate their prices. Rippling publishes one figure, a floor of $8 per employee per month plus a monthly base fee. For Workday, UKG, Paylocity, Paycom and Insperity, the vendor page also cites a public document that shows a price (listed below), attributed to its publisher.
