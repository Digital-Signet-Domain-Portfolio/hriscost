How to audit a specific figure

Click any source URL above to open the vendor's pricing page in a new tab. Compare the figure shown on our PEPM benchmark page or vendor page to the published list (where available) or to the buying-committee estimate range we disclose. If a figure has drifted since our capture date, file a correction at oliver@digitalsignet.com and we will re-verify within 14 days.

Chain-of-custody for quote-only vendors

For the 13 quote-only vendors in our coverage (Rippling, BambooHR, HiBob, Paylocity, Paycom, Workday, ADP WFN, UKG Pro, Namely, Sapling-Kallidus, TriNet Zenefits, Insperity, Paycor), the PEPM band is our own buying-committee estimate, not a quoted rate. We capture the vendor pricing page (typically a quote form) to confirm no list rate is published, then estimate a low-mid-high band from the vendor's stated pricing model and the observed market range for that archetype and seat band. Each quote-only band is flagged with the crimson "quote-only" provenance badge throughout the site so it is never mistaken for a published figure.
