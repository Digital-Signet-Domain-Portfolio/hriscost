Rippling vs BambooHR in 2026

Rippling publishes a floor: plans start at $8 per employee per month plus a monthly base fee, with every module quoted. BambooHR publishes full tiers: $10, $17 and $25 per employee per month for Core, Pro and Elite above 25 employees (both pricing pages read 24 November 2026). Rippling wins when IT/Device provisioning is in scope; BambooHR when the buyer wants a published price to budget from.

List cost = published per-employee rate x 100 x 12, plus any published monthly base fee x 12, before volume discounts and add-ons. Where a vendor publishes no price, enter its quote under “Your quote” in the ledger to compare the two over 3 years with your own add-ons, implementation fee and renewal cap.

Decision aid

Pick Rippling when IT/Device is a binding decision factor (laptop provisioning, SSO/SCIM at scale, app management). Rippling sells IT/Device as its own module; BambooHR has no equivalent.

Pick BambooHR when the buyer wants a published tier price ($17 per employee per month for Pro) rather than a quote for every module.

PEPM comparison at 100 employees

On published figures only: Rippling from $8 x 100 = $800 a month plus a base fee Rippling does not publish; BambooHR Pro $17 x 100 = $1,700 a month before volume discounts and before the quoted Payroll add-on. Neither is the all-in number: Rippling modules and BambooHR add-ons are both quoted.

Module scope differences

Rippling owns IT/Device natively; BambooHR does not. Rippling prices Recruiting and Performance as quoted modules; BambooHR groups features into its Core, Pro and Elite tiers, so compare tier contents on its pricing page.

Easier to migrate from Gusto?

Neither publishes a migration timeline or fee. Ask each how it imports your Gusto data, whether it runs a parallel payroll before cut-over, and whether migration is inside the implementation fee.

When pick BambooHR specifically?

When IT and device management are not in scope and the buyer wants a published price. BambooHR's tiers make that budget simple to set.
