Gusto vs Rippling in 2026

Gusto publishes its prices: Simple $49 a month + $6 per person, Plus $80 + $12, Premium $180 + $22, with free account setup. Rippling publishes one figure, plans starting at $8 per employee per month plus a monthly base fee, and quotes the base fee and every module. So the Gusto side of the comparison is on the page and the Rippling side needs a quote. The usual reason to move from Gusto to Rippling is scope: IT and device management, and recruiting and performance on the same platform.

List cost = published per-employee rate x 100 x 12, plus any published monthly base fee x 12, before volume discounts and add-ons. Where a vendor publishes no price, enter its quote under “Your quote” in the ledger to compare the two over 3 years with your own add-ons, implementation fee and renewal cap.

Decision aid

Pick Gusto when you want a published price, self-serve setup and payroll with HR basics, and are content to broker benefits separately if needed.

Pick Rippling when IT and device management, recruiting and performance need to be on one platform and you can work from a sales quote.

At what headcount does Rippling beat Gusto on cost?

It cannot be worked out from published prices, because Rippling publishes only its $8 floor and not its base fee or module rates. Price Gusto from its page, get a Rippling quote for the same modules at your headcount, and compare both in the ledger.

What is in each Gusto plan?

Gusto lists the features of Simple, Plus and Premium on its pricing page; HR tools such as performance and time tracking sit on Plus and Premium. Rippling sells Core HR plus modules (Payroll, IT, Recruiting, Performance and others), each priced in the quote.

Which has lower setup cost?

Gusto's pricing page states free account setup. Rippling publishes no implementation fee; ask for it in the quote.

Global-employment story?

Gusto pays contractors abroad with no monthly per-contractor fee on its standard plans. Rippling sells global payroll and EOR as quoted products. For an international team, price Rippling's global products against Deel or Remote, which publish theirs.
