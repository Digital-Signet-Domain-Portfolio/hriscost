HRIS integration and connector cost

The integration platforms are where this line item has published prices. Merge publishes its Launch plan at $650 a month for up to 10 production linked accounts (the first 3 free, then $65 per linked account above 10), and Finch publishes its Starter plan at $65 per connection per month for up to 15 connections. Workato publishes no price. Both pricing pages were read on 26 November 2026. Connector charges from the HRIS vendors themselves are set in their quotes, so ask for each integration as its own line.

Who pays these fees

Merge and Finch sell to software companies that build HR and payroll integrations into their own products: Finch describes Starter as for developers, and Merge describes Launch as for early-stage startups building a proof of concept. A company buying an HRIS usually meets them indirectly, inside a benefits, equity or payroll tool that reads its HRIS data, and pays for them only if it builds its own integration. Workato is an integration platform a company can buy to connect its own systems.

Worked example: building your own integration

At 10 connected systems, Merge Launch costs $650 x 12 = $7,800 a year, and Finch Starter costs 10 x $65 x 12 = $7,800 a year. At 3 or fewer, Merge Launch is free; from 4 to 9, Finch Starter costs less, because it charges per connection while Merge's $650 covers up to 10. These are platform fees only, before the engineering time to build and maintain the integration.

What to get quoted from the HRIS vendor

  • Which integrations are included in the plan (accounting, benefits carriers, identity provider, applicant tracking, Slack) and which carry a charge.
  • Whether API access is included or sold as a separate line or plan.
  • Any setup fee for each connector, and whether it sits inside the implementation fee.
  • Who maintains the connector when the other system changes its API.

Building the year-1 integration line

Year-1 integration cost = connector charges in the HRIS quote + setup fees for each connector + any integration platform fee x 12 + the subscription for the other system if you do not already have it. Enter the total as an add-on in the ledger, and its one-time parts with the implementation fee.
