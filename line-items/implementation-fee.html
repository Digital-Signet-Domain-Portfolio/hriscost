HRIS implementation cost in 2026

Few of the 20 HRIS vendors on hriscost.com publish an implementation price. Paycor's small-business page lists a payroll setup fee for each plan ($59 on Basic, $99 on Essential, $199 on Complete), waived under its current offer, and Justworks states it charges no implementation fee; the rest set implementation in the quote. So for most vendors the implementation line has to come from your quote: ask for it as a fixed fee with a written scope, then enter it in the ledger to see it beside 3 years of subscription.

What moves the fee

  • Headcount and entities: more employees, legal entities and pay groups mean more records to load and more configuration.
  • Modules: each module taken (payroll, benefits, time, recruiting, performance) is configured and tested separately.
  • Payroll history and parallel runs: moving payroll mid-year means loading year-to-date figures, and most buyers run old and new payroll side by side before cutting over.
  • Countries and states: each country, and each US state you withhold tax in, adds setup.
  • Integrations: connections to finance, benefits carriers, identity and other systems (see integration costs).

How to get the fee quoted so it can be compared

  • Ask for a fixed fee with a written scope and a list of what is out of scope, rather than a day-rate estimate.
  • Ask whether data migration and parallel payroll runs are inside the fee.
  • Ask whether the fee is waived or reduced for a longer term or annual prepayment, and get any waiver in the order form.
  • For enterprise platforms, ask whether the vendor or a partner firm implements, and price that contract alongside the subscription.
