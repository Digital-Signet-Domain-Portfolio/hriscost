The HRIS SSO upgrade tax

The “SSO tax” is the practice of selling SAML single sign-on only on a higher plan or for an extra charge; sso.tax keeps a list of vendors that do it. None of the HRIS vendors on this site is on that list, and none of the pricing pages we read on 26 November 2026 (BambooHR, Justworks, Rippling, Deel and Paycor) says which plan includes SAML SSO. So whether an HRIS charges you for SSO is set in the quote: ask for SSO and SCIM provisioning by name.

How to work out what SSO costs you

If the vendor includes SAML SSO in the plan you were going to buy anyway, it costs nothing extra. If it is only on a higher plan, the cost of SSO is the gap between the two plans: (per-employee rate of the plan with SSO minus the rate of the plan you need otherwise) x employees x 12, plus any difference in monthly base fee. If it is a separate line, it is that line. Enter the result as an add-on in the ledger to see it over 3 years.

SCIM provisioning

SCIM creates, updates and removes user accounts automatically from your identity provider, so a leaver loses access when HR records the exit. It is a separate capability from SSO login. Ask whether SCIM is included with SSO, sold with a different plan or module, or not offered.

What to put in the quote request

  • Name SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning, and your identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace).
  • Ask which plan includes each, and for any charge as its own line rather than folded into a tier upgrade.
  • Where SSO sits on a higher tier, ask for the tier price for your headcount so you can work out the gap.
  • Get the answer in the order form, so it carries through renewal.
