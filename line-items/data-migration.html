HRIS data migration cost

No HRIS vendor on this site publishes a data migration price; it is set in the implementation quote. Two quotes that public bodies published show what the line looks like. Paylocity's October 2025 quote to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District priced history imports at $1,000 each (three of them for 112 employees: check detail, department/position and pay rate), with the current-year check detail import included. Paycom's 2026 proposal to Columbia County, Oregon carries a one-time client conversion fee of $5,801.52 for 210 employees. Each is one buyer's quote, not a list price.

Each is one buyer's quote at its headcount and scope. Your own quote will differ with the history you load and the modules you take. Justworks states on its pricing page that it charges no implementation fee.

Fields that break

Historical time-off accruals, benefits enrolment dates, equity grant dates, employment-status changes and the dates of past pay changes are the records most likely to need fixing by hand, because each system stores them differently. Ask the vendor which of these its import covers, and budget your own team's time to check them.

What to get quoted

  • Whether migration is inside the implementation fee or its own line, as in both quotes above.
  • How many years of payroll history are loaded, and the price per history import.
  • Whether the current year's year-to-date figures are loaded (needed to switch payroll mid-year).
  • Who cleans the data before it is loaded: you, the vendor or a partner.

Parallel-payroll period cost

Most buyers run old and new payroll side by side for one or more pay cycles before cutting over. The cost is the old vendor's charges for those cycles plus your payroll team's checking time. Where a vendor charges per payroll run, it is: (per-run fee + per-check fee x employees) x parallel cycles. Paycom's Columbia County proposal shows that shape, a base charge per payroll run plus a charge per check. Use your current vendor's invoice for the figures.
