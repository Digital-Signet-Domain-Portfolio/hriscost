HRIS per-employee-per-month rates, 2026

Of the 20 vendors on hriscost.com, the ones that publish an HRIS seat rate list it at $3 to $29 per employee per month: from Zoho People Essential HR at $3 per user (US line-up, billed annually) to Deel HR Elite at $29. PEO plans sit higher because they include benefits administration: Justworks lists PEO Basic at $79 and PEO Plus at $124. 11 vendors, including Rippling, HiBob, Paylocity, Paycom, ADP, UKG and Workday, publish no per-employee rate on their own site; Rippling publishes only a floor of $8 plus a monthly base fee, and UKG's list prices appear in a reseller's state-contract price book (UKG Pro Pay and People Center at $22 per employee per month).

We do not put a number on a price a vendor keeps private. To compare one of these vendors, get its quote with each module, the base fee and the implementation fee as separate lines, then enter it under “Your quote” in the ledger.

Comparing a base-plus-seat price with a pure seat price

Some vendors add a monthly base fee to the per-employee rate (Gusto, Paychex Flex, Paycor, Justworks Payroll). To compare them with a pure per-employee price, divide the base fee by headcount and add it to the rate. Gusto Plus at 50 employees: $12 + $80 / 50 = $13.60 per employee per month. The base fee matters less as headcount grows.

PEO plans (Justworks PEO, Insperity) are not like-for-like with an HRIS seat: the per-employee figure includes payroll, HR services and benefits administration, and the PEO becomes co-employer.
