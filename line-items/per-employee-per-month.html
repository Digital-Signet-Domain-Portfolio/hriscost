HRIS per-employee-per-month benchmark, 2026

The 2026 PEPM range across the 20 vendors on hriscost.com runs from $1.50 (Zoho People Essential HR) to $140+ (Insperity PEO admin fee). Published-list SMB vendors cluster at $4-$10. Mid-market vendors land $10-$25. Enterprise platforms (Workday, UKG Pro) run $25-$45. PEO admin fees (Justworks, Insperity) sit in a separate band because the headline figure bundles benefits administration.

How to read this

The mid-PEPM column is the central buying-committee estimate. The low and high columns reflect the observed band: low for a Core-only deployment without modules, high for a full-suite deployment with all available add-ons. For published vendors (Gusto, Justworks, Paychex Flex, Zoho People, Deel, Remote, Oyster) the values cite the vendor's pricing page. For quote-only vendors the values are our own buying-committee estimates, derived from the vendor's stated pricing model and the observed market range for that archetype and seat band, and flagged as estimates rather than published list rates.

Apply a + 12% annual uplift across years 2 and 3 to model the typical 3-year contract economics. Across 3 years that compounds to roughly a 25% increase on the year-1 PEPM line.

PEPM normalisation across bundled vs modular vendors

The PEPM line is not directly comparable across vendor archetypes without normalisation. Three rules of thumb:

  • For pure-PEPM vendors (Rippling, BambooHR, HiBob, Paylocity), the headline figure is the PEPM line itself.
  • For base-plus-PEPM vendors (Gusto, Paychex Flex, Paycor), add base / headcount to PEPM at the relevant scale. Gusto Plus at 50 employees: $12 + $80/50 = $13.60 effective PEPM.
  • For PEO vendors (Justworks, Insperity), the headline PEPM includes payroll, comp, HR support, and benefits administration. Strip the benefits-administration value to normalise against a pure HRIS PEPM.
