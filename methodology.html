Methodology / hris cost methodology

How this ledger is built

hriscost.com is built as a ledger. Every rate on every page comes from one data file (src/data/vendors.ts) that holds, per vendor, the list rates and monthly base fees it publishes, the pricing page they come from and the date we read it. Where a vendor publishes no price, the file holds no price. The 3-year calculator is plain arithmetic that runs in your browser.

The ledger formula, cell by cell

For a chosen plan and headcount, the ledger computes:

  • Year 1 per-employee rate = the plan's published list rate + any add-on rate you enter
  • Year 1 subscription = per-employee rate x headcount x 12 + published monthly base fee x 12 (for a plan with a flat monthly price at small headcounts, the flat price x 12 replaces the per-employee charge at or below its limit)
  • Year 1 total = year 1 subscription + the implementation fee you enter
  • Year 2 and year 3 = year 1 subscription x (1 + your renewal increase), then x (1 + increase)²
  • 3-year total = the three years added; per employee per month over 3 years = 3-year total / (headcount x 36)

Add-ons, implementation and renewal increase all start at zero. Few vendors here publish them as a rate (Justworks lists its add-ons and Paycor its small-business setup fees, on their vendor pages), so the ledger takes them from your quote or contract rather than supplying a figure of its own.

Vendors that publish no price

Eleven of the 20 vendors (Rippling, HiBob, Paylocity, Paycom, Workday, ADP Workforce Now, UKG Pro, Namely, Sapling, TriNet and Insperity) publish no per-employee rate on their own site. We do not estimate one: a guess at a price a vendor keeps private cannot be checked, so it would read as a fact we do not have. Their pages give what they do publish (Rippling's $8 floor plus a base fee), what to get priced in writing, and, where one exists, a public document that shows a price: Workday's UK G-Cloud price list, a UKG reseller's state-contract MSRP list, quotes that Paylocity and Paycom gave public bodies and that those bodies published, and Insperity's per-employee results. Each is attributed to the document it comes from and dated. To model one of them, enter its quote under “Your quote” in the ledger.

Renewal increases

No vendor here publishes its renewal increase; it is set by each contract. The ledger therefore starts the increase at 0% and lets you set it to the cap in your contract. The renewal page shows what different increases do to a 3-year total.

Re-reading the sources

Each vendor's pricing page is re-read when that vendor's page on this site is reviewed, and the date shown beside every rate is the date it was last read. The /sources page lists every pricing page with that date; the /changelog page logs the price changes we have recorded.

What the ledger excludes

  • Benefits broker fees, which depend on your broker contract.
  • Health plan premiums for PEO buyers (pass-through, not vendor revenue).
  • Internal People-Ops staff time for implementation and ongoing administration.
  • Integration middleware (Merge.dev, Finch, Workato), covered separately on /line-items/integration-fees.
  • Volume discounts, which vendors apply in the quote and do not publish as a schedule.

Editorial standards

hriscost.com is published by Digital Signet and is editorially independent. No vendor pays for placement, no rates are influenced by affiliate relationships. Where an affiliate link is used, it is disclosed inline on the page where it appears. Corrections welcomed at oliver@digitalsignet.com.
